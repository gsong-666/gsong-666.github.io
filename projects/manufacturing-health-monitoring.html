02 / Edge AI · Intelligent manufacturing

Manufacturing Health Monitoring

A side-channel energy auditing framework for real-time health, security, and anomaly monitoring in robotic and additive-manufacturing processes.

  • Edge Computing
  • Energy Auditing
  • Robotics
  • 3D Printing
EDGE / AI

Challenge

Monitor equipment without disrupting production.

Manufacturing systems need rapid detection of abnormal behavior, but adding specialized internal sensors can be costly, intrusive, or difficult to retrofit.

Approach

Use energy consumption as a side-channel signal.

Electrical behavior is logged during operation and analyzed by anomaly-detection models deployed near the machine for responsive, in-situ monitoring.

System / 01

Data acquisition through edge deployment

  1. 01

    Energy logging

    A Raspberry Pi records side-channel energy-consumption data during robotic operation.

  2. 02

    Process integration

    OctoPrint and ROS are integrated on the Raspberry Pi to observe the operating state of a 3D printer.

  3. 03

    Real-time inference

    Anomaly-detection models run on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin for edge-based health monitoring.

Applications / 02

One monitoring principle, multiple manufacturing systems

The research develops a common energy-auditing perspective across robotic manipulators and additive-manufacturing equipment.

Robot operation

Manipulator anomaly detection

Learn normal energy patterns and detect deviations associated with abnormal operation.

Additive manufacturing

In-situ 3D-printing monitoring

Combine process-state information with energy signals for real-time equipment health assessment.
